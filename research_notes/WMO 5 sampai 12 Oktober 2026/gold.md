# China gold reserve purchases and XAUUSD, 5–12 October 2026

## What did China announce inside the requested week?

### Takeaway
SAFE's 7 October release establishes an acceleration in China's reported gold accumulation. Describe it as an addition to official gold reserves, not a buyback, and explicitly identify September as the observation month. The publication belongs to this week's news; the purchases did not necessarily occur this week.

### Cited Findings
- SAFE's original official-reserves table explicitly labels columns 2026.08 and 2026.09. Gold quantity increased from 76.73m to 77.47m fine troy ounces. Calculated increase: 740,000 oz, approximately 23.02 tonnes, versus August's 650,000 oz; approximately 13.85% acceleration. Calculated total: 2,409.59 tonnes. Gold's USD reserve valuation simultaneously fell from US$350.080bn to US$323.517bn, approximately 7.59%. Quantity and valuation must not be confused. — [SAFE original 2026 table](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2026/0206/27116.html)
- SAFE's original release explicitly states publication 2026-10-07 and September observation. FX reserves were US$3.4003trn, down US$38.1bn or 1.11%; SAFE attributes the decline to currency translation and asset-price changes. — [SAFE October 7 release](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2026/1006/27959.html)
- Reuters's October 7 story independently repeats the gold ounces and values, describes 23 consecutive months of accumulation, and quotes spot gold approximately US$4,147.47/oz at 02:44 GMT. Original syndicated body has the full 7 October 2026 publication date. — [Reuters via Mining Weekly](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/chinas-central-bank-adds-to-gold-reserves-for-twenty-third-consecutive-month-2026-10-07)

### Inferences
- More official demand is supportive of gold, but increasing reserves alone does not show that PBOC caused this week's spot-price movement, nor disclose purchase price or exact trade dates.
- An interesting contradiction: quantity increased while dollar value declined. This demonstrates valuation pressure can overwhelm incremental physical buying; it does not imply China sold gold.
- Preferred title: "China menambah emas, tetapi pembeli resmi belum menentukan arah XAUUSD."
