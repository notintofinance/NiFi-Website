# China payments market: banks, fintech wallets and clearing rails

## What are the best official series, and what do they actually measure?

### Takeaway
There is no single official “bank versus fintech transaction market share.” The PBOC publishes several **layers of the same payment stack**: bank-handled electronic payments, non-bank payment-institution network payments, bank-card transactions, and transactions cleared by UnionPay, NetsUnion (网联) and other systems. A wallet payment funded from a bank account can appear in more than one layer, so these series are excellent growth indicators but must not be added into a purported market total.

### Cited Findings
- **PBOC definition—bank electronic payment (银行电子支付):** transactions initiated from a settlement account through online banking, telephone banking, mobile banking, ATMs, POS and other electronic channels. “Mobile payment” is the subset initiated through mobile devices from bank settlement accounts. The 2023 report gives these definitions in footnote 11 on p.4. — [PBOC, *2023年支付体系运行总体情况*, published 28 Mar 2024, p.4](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/zhifujiesuansi/128525/128545/128643/e65053157ab14b9abf06bc9b18badb3a/2024032815235914509.pdf)
- **PBOC definition—non-bank network payment (非银行支付机构网络支付):** payment-institution transactions involving bank accounts plus network transactions using payment accounts. From 2023, the revised trial statistics include transfers uniformly and deduplicate inter-institution cooperation. — [PBOC, *2023年支付体系运行总体情况*, footnote 12, p.4](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/zhifujiesuansi/128525/128545/128643/e65053157ab14b9abf06bc9b18badb3a/2024032815235914509.pdf)
- **The overlap is explicit:** the non-bank series includes transactions “involving bank accounts,” while the bank series records account-changing transactions from settlement accounts. Therefore a consumer payment can be visible at the wallet/interface layer, the bank funding-account layer and the clearing layer; it is not valid to add all observations as unique purchases. — [PBOC, definitions in footnotes 11–13, *2023年支付体系运行总体情况*, p.4](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/zhifujiesuansi/128525/128545/128643/e65053157ab14b9abf06bc9b18badb3a/2024032815235914509.pdf)
- **PBOC definition—payment systems:** the 2023 aggregate includes PBOC clearing systems, commercial-bank intrabank systems, UnionPay, city/rural clearing, CIPS and NetsUnion. It mixes retail and wholesale flows, so its RMB value is not a retail-payment denominator. — [PBOC, footnote 13, *2023年支付体系运行总体情况*, p.4](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/zhifujiesuansi/128525/128545/128643/e65053157ab14b9abf06bc9b18badb3a/2024032815235914509.pdf)
- **PBOC’s 2010 baseline:** commercial banks processed 8.498bn online-payment transactions worth RMB522.39tn, 0.520bn telephone-payment transactions worth RMB9.44tn, and 0.118bn mobile-payment transactions worth RMB0.57tn; the report separately says the PBOC large- and small-value systems processed RMB1,120.58tn. — [PBOC, *中国支付体系发展报告（2010）*, published 20 Jul 2011, pp.32–34](https://www.gov.cn/gzdt/att/att/site1/20110720/1c6f6506c2380f90ec390e.pdf)
- **Early non-bank estimates are not a continuous PBOC series:** Analysys estimated third-party online-payment value at RMB1.0857tn in 2010 and RMB2.161tn in 2011; these are market-research estimates for online payment, not the later PBOC “network payment” definition. — [Sina/Analysys, 27 May 2011](https://news.sina.com.cn/c/2011-05-27/162322542768.shtml); [Internet Society of China/Analysys, 21 Feb 2012](https://www.isc.org.cn/article/19006.html)

### Inferences
- The best investment interpretation is **fintech won transaction frequency and the consumer interface; banks retained the high-value balance sheet and settlement substrate**. In 2025, non-banks recorded 4.15 times bank electronic-payment volume, while banks recorded 10.73 times non-bank payment value; these are activity ratios, not unique market shares. — [2025 PBOC figures reproduced by Cuiwei, 28 Mar 2026](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-28/1225042491.PDF)
- Calculated average ticket fell from about RMB41,700 for bank electronic payments versus RMB601 for non-bank network payments in 2013 to about RMB11,332 versus RMB255 in 2025. This strongly supports “bank = fewer/higher-value flows, fintech = small/high-frequency flows,” while recognizing that each aggregate contains heterogeneous transaction types. — [PBOC 2013, p.4](https://www.gov.cn/gzdt/att/att/site1/20140217/782bcb8883ce146be61101.pdf); [2025 PBOC figures reproduced by Cuiwei, 28 Mar 2026](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-28/1225042491.PDF)

### Gaps
- A direct PBOC URL for the full-year 2025 report was not indexed in the searches; the figures above are quoted consistently by listed-company filings and payment-industry reproductions, but the final report should replace the citation with the PBOC PDF when available.
- PBOC does not publish a clean funding-source split for wallet transactions (bank card/account versus payment-account balance versus consumer credit), so the portion economically funded by bank deposits cannot be quantified from public payment statistics.
- PBOC does not publish a single nationwide consumer retail-payment denominator that assigns each purchase exclusively to “bank front end” or “fintech front end.”

## What “market share” can be reported without double counting?

### Takeaway
Use four separate views: (1) wallet/front-end share, (2) normalized activity shares with a warning, (3) clearing-rail share, and (4) funding/custody share. Only the first and third have reasonably clean denominators; neither is the same thing as “conventional bank versus fintech.”

### Inferences
- For a **retail interface** statement, report the Alipay/WeChat duopoly share below. For **economy-wide money movement**, report bank electronic-payment value and wholesale rails, but do not call that consumer share. For **processing infrastructure**, report UnionPay/NetsUnion shares. These answer different investment questions and can coexist. — [BIS on overlay front ends using existing settlement systems, Mar 2020](https://www.bis.org/publ/qtrpdf/r_qt2003f.pdf); [PBOC system definitions, 2023](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/zhifujiesuansi/128525/128545/128643/e65053157ab14b9abf06bc9b18badb3a/2024032815235914509.pdf)
- The 2024 rail data show why the simple binary fails: NetsUnion carried roughly three quarters of the two networks’ transactions and two thirds of value, yet these flows commonly debit/credit commercial-bank accounts; banks remain balance-sheet counterparties even when NetsUnion and a fintech wallet own routing and interface. — [PBOC 2025 Financial Stability Report, table 24](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/goutongjiaoliu/113456/113469/2025122616592613805/2025122616590775273.pdf)

### Gaps
- There is no audited public series splitting UnionPay volume among bank-app/physical-card, Cloud QuickPass, and payment-institution-initiated QR transactions.
- There is no official wallet-front-end transaction table including bank apps, UnionPay QuickPass, Alipay and WeChat Pay on one mutually exclusive basis.

## What were Alipay and Tenpay/WeChat Pay shares over time?

### Takeaway
The defensible conclusion is not a single timeless percentage: Alipay and Tenpay/WeChat jointly rose from roughly two thirds to three quarters of third-party **online** payment in 2010–2011, to roughly 90–95% of third-party **mobile/wallet** transactions from the late 2010s through 2026. Vendor estimates differ because online payment, mobile payment, wallet use, transaction value and transaction volume are different denominators.

- Ant’s 2020 prospectus reported more than one billion Alipay users, 80 million merchants and RMB118tn domestic TPV in the 12 months ended 30 Jun 2020; it also states the platform served as a gateway to partner banks, asset managers and insurers. — [Ant Group HK prospectus, filed 26 Oct 2020](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2020/1026/2020102600165.pdf)
- CNNIC’s broader network-payment-user series rose from 166.76m people/32.5% of internet users in Dec 2011 to 1.029bn/92.8% in Dec 2024. This measures usage penetration, not an exclusive provider share. — [CNNIC 2012 report, figure 23](https://www.cnnic.cn/NMediaFile/old_attach/P020120723477451202474.pdf); [CNNIC 55th report, figure 27](https://www3.cnnic.cn/NMediaFile/2025/0313/MAIN17418452848150SDUMQZGSU.pdf)

### Inferences
- Report **“about 90–95% of third-party/mobile-wallet transactions since the late 2010s”** as the defensible duopoly range. Do not say Alipay and WeChat control 90–95% of all Chinese payments, since wholesale transfers, bank intrabank transfers, bank cards, cash and e-CNY sit outside that denominator. — [IMF 2026 note](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/005/2026/009/article-A001-en.xml); [PBOC 2023 system definitions](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/zhifujiesuansi/128525/128545/128643/e65053157ab14b9abf06bc9b18badb3a/2024032815235914509.pdf)
- WeChat’s social/red-packet use case rapidly converted a highly asymmetric 2014 market into a stable duopoly by 2016–2018; Alipay remained somewhat stronger in transaction value, while WeChat had a frequency/social-network advantage. — [BIS working paper on the 2014 red-packet turning point](https://www.bis.org/publ/work947.pdf); [IMF WP 23/123, p.10](https://www.imf.org/-/media/Files/Publications/WP/2023/English/wpiea2023123-print-pdf.ashx)

### Gaps
- No regulator publishes audited annual provider-level Alipay versus Tenpay volume/value. Post-2020 company-specific splits are mostly proprietary vendor estimates or surveys.
- “Tenpay” can include WeChat Pay and QQ Wallet; studies do not always state whether both are included. Provider labels and scope must be preserved exactly.
- Recent public estimates rarely distinguish P2P transfers, red packets, merchant purchases, credit-card repayment and wealth transfers, which can materially change shares.

## How do banks and fintechs coexist operationally and economically?

### Takeaway
Fintechs generally own discovery, QR initiation, merchant/user experience and data; banks continue to supply deposit accounts, cards, credit, KYC, settlement liquidity and licensed financial products. Regulation since 2017 has made the shared infrastructure more explicit: payment institutions cannot act as unregulated cross-bank clearing systems, and most bank-linked wallet traffic must pass through UnionPay or NetsUnion.

### Cited Findings
- The 2016 PBOC rectification plan required payment institutions’ cross-bank payments to pass through a PBOC system or licensed clearing institution and called for ending multiple direct bank connections. — [PBOC, *非银行支付机构风险专项整治工作实施方案*, 13 Oct 2016](https://zhengzhou.pbc.gov.cn/zhengzhou/124251/3167821/3169681/index.html)
- PBOC required payment institutions to raise reserve centralization to 100% by 14 Jan 2019, close most commercial-bank reserve accounts, and move centralized-reserve transfers through UnionPay or NetsUnion. — [PBOC notice 银办发〔2018〕114号, 29 Jun 2018, pp.7–8](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/chubanwu/114566/114579/4356045/4356177/2021100915270894643.pdf)
- By 31 Dec 2018, 99% of bank–payment-institution cooperative payments previously processed bilaterally had moved to UnionPay/NetsUnion; PBOC described the reform as improving reserve safety, monitoring, efficiency and cost. — [PBOC policy briefing, 10 Jan 2019](https://xzxk.pbc.gov.cn/opal/zwfwqjd/zwfwqjd-news001.html)
- In 2018 NetsUnion had connected more than 400 banks and all 115 licensed network-payment institutions, including Alipay and Tenpay/WeChat Pay. — [Cyberspace Administration of China, 3 Dec 2018](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2018-12/03/c_1123797910.htm)
- Ant disclosed that users choose among wallet balances and other funding sources, that merchant payment revenue is primarily a percentage of TPV, and that it also charges for transfers to bank accounts and credit-card repayments. — [Ant Group prospectus, 2020, business overview](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1577552/000110465920082881/a20-6321_46k.pdf)
- Ant’s 2019 revenue mix shows why payments became a gateway rather than the whole profit pool: digital payments and merchant services were RMB51.905bn/43.0% of revenue, while CreditTech, InvestmentTech and InsureTech together were RMB67.784bn/56.2%. — [Ant Group prospectus, 2020, summary table](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1577552/000110465920082881/a20-6321_46k.pdf)
- The prospectus states partner financial institutions funded all but about 2% of the consumer and SMB credit balance enabled on Ant’s platform as of Jun 2020; Ant supplied customer reach, risk technology and distribution. — [Ant Group prospectus, footnote 7 and business overview, 2020](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1577552/000110465920082881/a20-6321_46k.pdf)
- Regulators halted Ant’s IPO in Nov 2020 because regulatory interviews and fintech-rule changes could materially affect its business structure and profit model; from Nov 2020, regulators required Ant, Tencent and other large platforms to rectify financial activities. — [CSRC, 4 Nov 2020](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c1000674/content.shtml); [CSRC/PBOC joint regulatory statement, 7 Jul 2023](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c7418599/content.shtml)
- Interoperability reduced closed ecosystems: by late 2021 Alipay–UnionPay QR acceptance was nationwide and WeChat Pay–QuickPass interoperability had begun in provincial capitals; CNNIC says Alipay, WeChat Pay and QuickPass achieved offline QR interoperability by 2024. — [Beijing municipal government/Beijing Daily, 6 Dec 2021](https://www.beijing.gov.cn/fuwu/bmfw/sy/jrts/202112/t20211206_2553887.html); [CNNIC 55th report, Jan 2025](https://www3.cnnic.cn/n4/2025/0117/c88-11229.html)
- The 2023 State Council regulation, effective 1 May 2024, defines non-bank payment institutions as licensed non-banks that transfer funds on users’ instructions, directs bank cooperation, and says their purpose is small-value/convenient payment—not unlicensed clearing. — [PBOC/State Council Decree 768, 19 Dec 2023](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/144953/5174993/index.html)

### Inferences
- A wallet purchase can generate economics for several parties: the fintech can earn merchant/technology fees and data-enabled distribution revenue; the acquiring side/clearing network can earn processing fees; the issuing bank retains the deposit/card relationship, earns interchange or account economics and provides settlement liquidity. The “winner” depends on whether the investor values interface, fee take rate, data, deposits or credit assets. — [Ant prospectus on merchant fees and bank/financial-institution partners](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1577552/000110465920082881/a20-6321_46k.pdf); [PBOC clearing reform](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/chubanwu/114566/114579/4356045/4356177/2021100915270894643.pdf)
- Regulation shifted fintechs from quasi-clearing/balance-sheet expansion toward licensed technology and distribution partnerships, while forcing banks to modernize mobile apps, QR acceptance and API-style cooperation. The result is **co-opetition**, not replacement. — [PBOC 2016 rectification plan](https://zhengzhou.pbc.gov.cn/zhengzhou/124251/3167821/3169681/index.html); [CSRC/PBOC platform rectification statement, 7 Jul 2023](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c7418599/content.shtml)
- User behavior explains the coexistence: already in 2012, 79.2% of surveyed online-payment users used third-party account balances and 75.7% used online banking, demonstrating substantial multi-homing rather than exclusive substitution. — [CNNIC, *2012年中国网络支付安全状况报告*, 21 Nov 2012](https://www3.cnnic.cn/n4/2022/0401/c119-1026.html)

### Gaps
- Public disclosures do not provide the end-to-end fee split among wallet, acquirer, clearing network and issuing bank for a representative transaction; merchant discount rates also vary by segment.
- Tencent reports FinTech and Business Services together, not WeChat Pay revenue or TPV separately, which prevents an audited payment-only financial comparison with Ant.

## How should e-CNY and regulatory breaks be treated through 2026?

### Takeaway
e-CNY is a form of money and settlement asset, not simply another private payment company. It can appear inside bank, Alipay and WeChat interfaces; therefore its transaction value should be tracked separately and never added as a competing “fintech wallet market share” without identifying the interface and settlement layer.

### Cited Findings
- IMF explains that e-CNY is legal tender/a form of money, whereas Alipay and Tenpay are payment platforms; e-CNY can also be used through private platforms and can operate without a bank account. — [IMF, China Selected Issues, 2022](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/002/2022/022/article-A005-en.xml)
- End-2024: trial regions had processed a cumulative RMB9.4tn and 2.56bn e-CNY transactions; these are **cumulative pilot totals**, not annual retail TPV. — [PBOC, *中国普惠金融指标分析报告（2024–2025年）*, p.8](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/xindaishichangsi/5443861/5443865/5443892/5957f8d15a824e9e88d01710873482d3/2025110711361912922.pdf)
- End-Sep 2025: cumulative e-CNY activity reached RMB14.2tn and 3.32bn transactions with 225m personal app wallets across 26 localities in 17 provincial-level regions. — [State Council/Xinhua, 29 Oct 2025](https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/statistics/202510/29/content_WS6901a9c9c6d00ca5f9a0726a.html)
- From 1 Jan 2026, official reporting describes a move toward “digital deposit money”: balances in real-name commercial-bank wallets become commercial-bank liabilities paying interest and covered by deposit insurance; non-bank-held e-CNY guarantee funds are treated like payment-institution reserves. — [MOFCOM/China News Service, 30 Dec 2025](https://dzswgf.mofcom.gov.cn/news/43/2025/12/1767072924980.html)

- The 2026 deposit-liability redesign makes the bank role more explicit: a consumer may experience an e-CNY wallet while the regulated liability resides at a commercial bank, reinforcing the need to separate front end from balance sheet. — [MOFCOM/CNS, 30 Dec 2025](https://dzswgf.mofcom.gov.cn/news/43/2025/12/1767072924980.html)

### Gaps
- National annual e-CNY payment flow (not cumulative pilot activity) and front-end provider share are not publicly disclosed.
- The magnitude of e-CNY merchant payments versus government, intercompany and wholesale pilot flows is not separated in headline cumulative figures.

## What can Bloomberg Terminal add?

### Takeaway
Bloomberg is most valuable for audited company/bank financials, standardized consensus and segment histories—not for recreating PBOC payment statistics. The terminal request should explicitly separate listed-parent economics from private payment subsidiaries and request raw source/method metadata for any proprietary share series.

### Cited Findings
- Alibaba does not consolidate Ant’s operating data in the same way Tencent consolidates its fintech services, while Tencent’s disclosed segment combines FinTech and Business Services. Therefore Bloomberg standardized financials cannot by themselves produce an apples-to-apples Alipay-versus-WeChat Pay P&L. Ant’s last detailed public operating baseline remains the 2020 prospectus. — [Ant Group HK prospectus, 2020](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2020/1026/2020102600165.pdf); [Tencent 2024 annual report](https://static.www.tencent.com/uploads/2025/04/08/1132b72b565389d1b913aea60a648d73.pdf)

#### Precise Bloomberg data request template for the user

1. **Listed-company panel, annual 2010–2025 and latest 2026 YTD:** `700 HK Equity` (Tencent), `9988 HK Equity` and `BABA US Equity` (Alibaba; clearly flag Ant as equity-method/private), `1398 HK Equity` (ICBC), `3988 HK Equity` (Bank of China), `939 HK Equity` (CCB), `1288 HK Equity` (ABC), `3968 HK Equity` (CMB), `300773 CH Equity` (Lakala). Export reported currency, fiscal period and original filing link. Validate fields in `FLDS <GO>` before export.
2. **Core standardized fields:** `SALES_REV_TURN`, `OPER_INC`, `NET_INCOME`, `BS_TOT_ASSET`, `BS_CUSTOMER_DEPOSITS`, `BS_GROSS_LOANS`, `NET_INTEREST_INCOME`, `IS_COMM_AND_FEE_INC`, `CF_CAP_EXPEND`, `CUR_MKT_CAP`, `BEST_SALES`, `BEST_NET_INCOME`, `BEST_EPS`. Bloomberg field availability differs for banks; include each field’s terminal description/mnemonic from `FLDS` so the research can audit definitions.
3. **Segment data:** export Bloomberg segment history for Tencent “FinTech and Business Services,” Alibaba equity-method/Ant-related profit if separately disclosed, and each bank’s “bank card / settlement / agency / electronic banking” fee revenue where available. Include segment label, reported value, currency, fiscal year and footnote; do not map the whole Tencent segment to payments.
4. **Operating KPIs:** for Tencent, Weixin/WeChat combined MAU, commercial-payment volume/merchant commentary if present; for Alibaba/Ant, Alipay annual/monthly active users, merchants, TPV, digital-payment revenue, CreditTech/InvestmentTech/InsureTech revenue; for banks, mobile-banking MAU, digital customers, mobile transaction count/value, cards in force, card spending and settlement/clearing fee income. These will often require filing-level Bloomberg `FA`/document exports rather than standardized fields.
5. **Private/industry series:** search Bloomberg Economics/Intelligence and third-party datasets for exact descriptions: “China third-party mobile payment transaction value share,” “Alipay market share,” “Tenpay market share,” “PBOC bank electronic payment,” “PBOC non-bank network payment,” “UnionPay clearing transaction value,” and “NetsUnion transaction value.” Export **series mnemonic, provider, definition, unit, frequency, observation date, publication/revision date and source URL**. If no metadata are available, do not use the number.
6. **Bank peer financial request:** net interest income, net fee and commission income, bank-card fee income, settlement/clearing fee income, customer deposits, retail deposits, retail loans, credit-card receivables/NPL, digital users and IT spending for the five banks above. This lets the final report test whether wallet growth reduced payment fees but improved deposit/credit distribution.

### Inferences
- Bloomberg can materially improve the investment case by connecting transaction growth to **monetization**: payment/merchant-services revenue, fee take, user/merchant growth, bank fee-income mix, deposit franchise and IT spend. It cannot fix the conceptual double counting in PBOC activity layers. — [Ant prospectus revenue table](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1577552/000110465920082881/a20-6321_46k.pdf); [PBOC 2023 definitions](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/zhifujiesuansi/128525/128545/128643/e65053157ab14b9abf06bc9b18badb3a/2024032815235914509.pdf)

### Gaps
- Exact Bloomberg mnemonics for PBOC payment-system series could not be verified without terminal access. The user should export the `ECST`/series-search results with identifiers rather than relying on guessed tickers.
- Ant Group, UnionPay and NetsUnion are not straightforward listed-equity comparables; audited private-company financial histories may remain unavailable even on Bloomberg.
